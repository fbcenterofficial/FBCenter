Scoring

Classic Scoring

Correct letter+5 pts
Wrong guess-2 pts
Word completed+20–50 pts

The word bonus grows with difficulty: Easy +20, Medium +30, Hard +40, Expert +50. Word Packs give +20.

Killer Scoring

Wrong guess (good!)+3 pts
Correct guess (bad!)-1 pts
Man hanged+5–20 pts

The hanged-man bonus grows with difficulty: Easy +5, Medium +10, Hard +15, Expert +20.

Daily Word Scoring

Correct letter+10 pts
Wrong guess-5 pts
Word completed+50 pts

Time Attack Scoring

Correct letter+5 pts
Wrong guess-2 pts
Word completed+20 pts

Same scoring as Classic — see how many words you can score before time runs out.

Survival Scoring

Same scoring as Classic for every word you guess, and the word bonus grows with the run's level (+20 up to +50). Mistakes add up across the whole run — after 6, the man is hanged. Your record (words guessed) shows on your Profile.

Anagram Scoring

Word solved: 12 points on Easy, 22 on Medium, 32 on Hard, 42 on Expert, plus 5 points per letter. Each wrong attempt takes 10% off (down to 25% at most). Hints don't cost points.

Word Packs

Word Packs uses the same scoring as Classic — just with a themed word list.

Weekly Challenge — Scoring

Correct letter: up to 90 points, split across however many letters you still need to find. Mistake: -10 points. Word completed: +50 bonus points. Sundays are a rest day with no competition.

Daily Missions

+50 XP for each mission you finish and a +100 XP bonus for finishing all 3 on the same day. New missions at midnight (UTC).
