Hangmania

A word-guessing game with actual depth. Four ways to play: Classic, a competitive "Killer Mode" that flips the rules on their head, a Daily Word with streaks, and Time Attack against the clock. Twelve themed word packs across six languages, difficulty tiers from Easy to Expert, and 51 achievements feeding into a 24-tier rank system that climbs all the way to King of Hangmania. Play solo, or start a Pass & Play round — hand one phone around the table, or play online across separate devices with a lobby code.

PayClock

An all-in-one workforce and personal finance app for iOS, built for businesses of any size. Admins build schedules, track hours and payroll, approve time-off requests, send real-time announcements, assign work tasks, and watch employee locations with geofenced clock-in/out. Employees clock in, submit time-off requests, track earnings on a monthly calendar, and log mileage for reimbursement. Everyone gets built-in personal finance tools — expenses, income, debts and loans, calendar notes. Multi-role dashboards, real-time cloud sync, biometric lock, CSV/PDF export, and support for 8 languages.
